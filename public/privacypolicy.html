Task Foundry Privacy Policy

Task Foundry is a habit / factory game. Progress is stored on your device (local save data) under your signed-in account.

Health & step data

On the native mobile app, with your permission, Task Foundry may read step counts from Apple Health (HealthKit) or Android Health Connect. That data is used only to power in-game mining cycles and progress. We do not sell health data. Step totals are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our servers by the app itself.

You can revoke health access at any time in system Settings (iOS Health / Android Health Connect).

Account

Optional Google sign-in uses Google Identity Services. We do not receive your Google password.
